# Car Spare Parts Management System

## 📌 Project Overview

The Car Spare Parts Management System is a web-based application designed to help a spare parts shop manage its daily operations efficiently.

The system will help the shop owner and employees manage inventory, record sales, track customer payments, manage suppliers, monitor business expenses, and calculate business profit.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Manage spare parts inventory efficiently.
- Record and track sales transactions.
- Manage customer information and payment balances.
- Manage suppliers and stock purchases.
- Track business expenses.
- Calculate business profit.
- Generate useful business reports.
- Improve the accuracy of shop records and daily operations.

## ✨ Main Features

### 1. Dashboard
- Display an overview of shop activities.
- Show total products and current stock levels.
- Display recent sales and purchases.
- Highlight low-stock products.
- Show outstanding customer and supplier balances.
- Display sales, expenses, and profit summaries.

### 2. Inventory Management
- Add, edit, view, and delete spare parts.
- Store product details, including:
  - Product name
  - Product code or SKU
  - Category
  - Car brand and model
  - Purchase price
  - Selling price
  - Available quantity
  - Minimum stock level
  - Supplier
  - Storage location
- Search and filter products.
- Automatically update stock after sales and purchases.
- Identify products that need restocking.

### 3. Sales Management
- Record sales transactions.
- Add multiple spare parts to a single sale.
- Automatically calculate the total sale amount.
- Reduce inventory quantities after a sale.
- Generate sales receipts.
- View sales history.
- Record returned items and process returns.

### 4. Payment Management
- Record full, partial, and unpaid transactions.
- Track customer payments.
- Calculate outstanding customer balances.
- Record supplier payments.
- Track outstanding supplier balances.
- View payment histories.

### 5. Customer Management
- Register and manage customer information.
- View customer purchase histories.
- Track customer payments and outstanding balances.
- Search for customers and their transactions.

### 6. Supplier Management
- Register and manage supplier information.
- View supplier details and supplied products.
- Track purchases from each supplier.
- Monitor supplier payments and outstanding balances.
- View supplier transaction histories.

### 7. Stock Purchase Management
- Record new stock purchases from suppliers.
- Add purchased quantities to inventory.
- Record purchase prices and dates.
- Track payment status for each purchase.
- Maintain purchase histories.

### 8. Profit and Expense Management
- Record business expenses, such as rent, electricity, transportation, and other operating costs.
- Categorize expenses.
- Record expense amounts, dates, and descriptions.
- View expense histories.
- Calculate sales revenue and the cost of goods sold.
- Calculate gross profit and net profit based on recorded sales and expenses.
- Generate profit and expense reports for selected periods.

### 9. Reports and Analytics
- Daily, weekly, and monthly sales reports.
- Inventory and stock reports.
- Low-stock reports.
- Stock purchase reports.
- Customer payment and outstanding balance reports.
- Supplier payment and outstanding balance reports.
- Business expense reports.
- Profit and loss reports.

### 10. User Management
- Allow authorized users to log in securely.
- Support different user roles, such as administrator and employee.
- Control access to features based on user permissions.
- Protect sensitive business information.

### 11. Notifications
- Notify users when products reach their minimum stock levels.
- Highlight outstanding customer payments.
- Highlight outstanding supplier payments.
- Display relevant business alerts.

### 12. System Settings
- Manage shop information.
- Configure receipt details.
- Set minimum stock levels.
- Manage user permissions.
- Configure other system preferences.

## 👥 Intended Users

The system is designed for internal shop operations:

- **Administrator:** Manages users, inventory, sales, purchases, expenses, reports, and system settings.
- **Employee:** Performs authorized daily tasks, such as recording sales, managing stock, and processing payments.

The system is intended for shop management, not public online shopping or customer ordering.

## 🎯 Expected Outcome

The system aims to provide a centralized platform for managing spare parts inventory, sales, purchases, customers, suppliers, payments, expenses, and business profitability.

It will help the shop reduce manual recordkeeping, improve inventory accuracy, monitor outstanding balances, and make better business decisions using reliable reports.
